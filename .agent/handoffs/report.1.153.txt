Sequence-step coverage approach
- Added explicit `action_coverage_refs` and `event_coverage_refs` to every documented AppState transition-sequence step.
- Each action stimulus step now points at its canonical action coverage record; each event stimulus step points at its canonical event coverage record. Steps with both action and event stimuli declare both refs; non-applicable coverage kind is an empty list.
- Extended the contract guard to validate sequence-step coverage ref shape, duplicates, unknown refs, wrong-kind refs, stimulus mismatches, transition mismatches, and runtime/docs drift for these refs.
- Extended runtime sequence-step metadata with action/event coverage ref arrays and startup readiness validation that resolves the refs through action/event coverage lookup before normal execution continues.

Residual risks / follow-up suggestions
- The new sequence-step refs prove the canonical action/event coverage record and transition linkage for each scripted step; they do not expand action/event coverage registries to enumerate every scenario-specific invariant or projection-only generation expectation.
