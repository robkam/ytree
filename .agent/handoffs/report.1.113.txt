## Auditor finding correction

The complete fixture now preserves invariant-to-surface specificity: dispatch surfaces are distributed across invariant categories instead of every invariant listing every surface. The new split-invariant docs and runtime regressions prove the required intersection semantics directly: a surface reference in one invariant and field protection in another is rejected because no single invariant provides both the target surface and allowed-write field coverage.
