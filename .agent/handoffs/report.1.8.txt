   Impact: The guard does not fully satisfy the requirement to catch malformed action list fields.    Concrete fix: For required list fields, require isinstance(value, list), non-empty, and every element to be a non-empty string. Add temporary-fixture tests that fail for action declared_write_set/migration_notes, transition declared_write_set/side_effects, and shim invariant_checks/migration_notes containing non-string or blank-string elements.
